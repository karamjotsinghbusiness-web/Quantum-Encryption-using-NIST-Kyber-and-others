# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, technically honest, and constructive. Critique claims and code
with evidence, not people. Welcome questions asked in good faith, including
beginner questions. Do not harass, threaten, demean, or discriminate against
other participants.

## Research and safety expectations

- Distinguish measurements from universal performance claims.
- Distinguish functional tests from security proofs or certification.
- Use synthetic data and never publish credentials, private keys, malware, or
  sensitive personal information.
- Cite primary sources for cryptographic and standards claims.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation for unacceptable behavior.
Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile. Do not publish private details in an issue or discussion.

Maintainers will review reports as promptly and fairly as possible and will
respect the reporter's privacy where practical.
